How it works

Why teaching Raju helps you learn

Raju makes mistakes Hindi speakers make in Telugu, such as adding a word for “is” (ఇది పుస్తకం ఉంది) because Hindi says यह किताब है. When you fix his sentence you have to recall the rule yourself, and explaining it makes it stick. When your memory of a rule starts to fade, Raju forgets it too and makes the mistake again, so your review comes as teaching.

An AI told to “act like a beginner” knows too much and gives answers away. So rules, not the AI, keep Raju one step behind you:

PlannerPicks a rule Raju does not know yet, more often the ones you get wrong.
RajuAn AI writes his sentence and checks it with a tool before you see it.
FilterOnly words you have met, exactly one mistake, no answer, no explaining.
RefereePlain rules, no AI, decide if your fix is right. Only a right fix teaches Raju.
MemoryTracks how well you remember each rule; below 50%, Raju forgets it too.

Study mode · for the research pilot

Three ways to practise, one learner

Each learner practises different rules in each of three conditions, rotated so every rule meets every condition across learners. Practice is matched: the same rules, the same number of exposures and the same feedback (a hint first, then the correct form). Tests give no feedback.
