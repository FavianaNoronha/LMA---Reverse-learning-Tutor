You already know most Telugu letters

How it works

You learn Telugu by teaching Raju

Raju is an AI classmate who makes the mistakes a Hindi speaker makes in Telugu, such as adding a word for “is” (ఇది పుస్తకం ఉంది) because Hindi says यह किताब है. You correct him. Fixing his sentence makes you recall the rule, explaining it makes it stick, and when your memory of a rule fades Raju forgets it too, so your review arrives as re-teaching.

An AI told to “act like a beginner” knows too much and leaks answers. So Raju is kept a step behind you by rules, not by the AI:

PlannerPicks a rule Raju does not know yet, favouring ones you get wrong.
Raju (AI agent)Drafts a sentence and calls a checking tool; revises if it is rejected.
Leak filterOnly words you have met, exactly one mistake, no answer, no explaining.
Referee (rules)Marks your fix correct, wrong or unsure. Only a correct fix teaches Raju.
MemoryTracks your recall; below 50%, Raju forgets the rule too.

Reading and typing. Most Hindi speakers cannot yet read or type Telugu, so every Telugu sentence also shows its Hindi letters (they fade after you get a rule right three times), there is a listen button, and you can type answers in Hindi letters: the tutor converts them to Telugu before checking, and forgives ए/ओ typed for Telugu's short ఎ/ఒ. The Letters page teaches the six things that are new for a Hindi reader. The same aids are on in every study condition.

About this version. This page runs entirely in your browser with the project's 15 draft Hindi→Telugu rules. Raju's drafts come from a scripted stand-in for the language model (every third conversation starts with a deliberately leaky draft, so you can see the checking tool reject it). The full system runs Raju on Gemma 3 12B through the same tool and filter. Your progress is kept only in this browser. The Telugu sentences are drafts awaiting native-speaker review. Telugu speakers learning Hindi is the next direction; its rules are being written.
