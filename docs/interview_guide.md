# Week-1 interviews (5–8 people, ~20 min each)

Who: adult learners of Telugu or Hindi, and Hindi/Telugu teachers or tutors.
Rule: ask about what they do **now** before showing anything. Record commitments, not compliments.
Consent: say how notes are used; no recording without a yes; no names in the notes file.

## Learners
1. When did you last need Telugu/Hindi and couldn't manage? What happened?
2. What have you tried (apps, classes, friends)? Why did you stop?
3. Which mistakes keep coming back even after someone corrects you?
4. Would correcting an AI classmate's sentences feel useful or artificial? Why?
5. Who would pay for this: you, your employer, your college? How much is a month of a class now?

## Teachers
1. How do you find and explain recurring mistakes now? How long does it take?
2. How do you know a student fixed a mistake and didn't just memorise one correction?
3. Would you trust an AI's error label after reviewing it? What would you need to check?
4. Who in your institution decides on and pays for tools?

## End with an ask (record yes/no)
- Try a 20-minute session next week?
- Share permitted, anonymised example sentences?
- Introduce one more learner or teacher?
